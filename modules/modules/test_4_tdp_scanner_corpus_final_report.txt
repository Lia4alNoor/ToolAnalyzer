2. MITIGATION COVERAGE FOR MATCHED COMPOSITIONS (MODULE 8)
==========================================================================

P1 - MCP-UPD / IPI Data Stealing
  Coverage status : Literature-supported
  Literature mitigations:
    - LM-1 Mandatory Access Control (MAC) [DIRECT] (explicitly evaluated/addressed by the source)
      papers: SEAgent | timing: Runtime / Pre-execution policy
      effectiveness: Source reports 0% ASR (100% of benchmarked attacks blocked) on the InjecAgent and AgentDojo benchmarks, versus up to 80% ASR for unprotected agents.
      limitations: Requires correct policy authoring; does not detect prompt-level manipulation; coarse policies may block benign behaviour.
    - LM-2 Least-Privilege ILP Solver [DIRECT] (potentially applicable (not empirically validated for this pattern))
      papers: MiniScope | timing: Pre-execution (deployment-time)
      effectiveness: Source reports optimal least-privilege scoping (versus LLM-based solvers with overprivilege ratios of 1.04-2.19) at 1-7% execution-latency overhead; no ASR-reduction value is reported.
      limitations: Cannot prevent misuse of capabilities that remain granted; security/utility trade-off.
    - LM-6 Pre-Execution Tool Filtering [DIRECT] (explicitly evaluated/addressed by the source)
      papers: AgentDojo | timing: Pre-execution (before tool exposure)
      effectiveness: Source reports targeted IPI ASR reduced from 57.69% to 6.84% on GPT-4o while maintaining 73.13% benign utility.
      limitations: Requires task-appropriate tool scoping; less effective when the attack only uses in-scope tools.
    - LM-7 Safety Fine-Tuning [DIRECT] (explicitly evaluated/addressed by the source)
      papers: InjecAgent | timing: Pre-deployment (training-time)
      effectiveness: Source reports fine-tuned GPT-4 ASR of 7.1% versus 47.0% for prompted ReAct GPT-4 (GPT-3.5: 8.4% versus 39.8%).
      limitations: Generalization to unseen attacks is not guaranteed; requires training access.
    - LM-8 Auxiliary ML Injection Detector [DIRECT] (explicitly evaluated/addressed by the source)
      papers: AgentDojo | timing: Pre-execution (on ingestion)
      effectiveness: Source reports targeted IPI ASR lowered from 57.69% to 7.95%, but benign task utility degraded from 69.0% to 41.49% due to false positives.
      limitations: High false-positive cost to benign utility; adaptive attacks may evade the detector.
    - LM-9 Data Delimiting / Spotlighting [DIRECT] (explicitly evaluated/addressed by the source)
      papers: AgentDojo; STAC | timing: Pre-execution (on ingestion)
      effectiveness: AgentDojo reports delimiting lowering targeted IPI ASR to 41.65% (benign utility 72.66%); STAC reports spotlighting had negligible effect against STAC (ASR 85.3% at turn T versus 87.4% with no defense).
      limitations: Models do not perfectly honour delimiters; evaluated against STAC with negligible effect, so it is not listed as a P2 mitigation.
  Project-derived mitigations:
    - PDM-2 Dynamic Outbound Argument Sandboxing (Argument-Level Access Control; Runtime / Pre-execution)
      evidence status: PROJECT-DERIVED — not directly evaluated by the cited literature
      rationale: Restricts sensitive outbound arguments such as recipients, destinations, domains, or paths when untrusted C1 content is present, synthesizing least privilege with argument-level policy enforcement.

P2 - Sequential Tool Attack Chaining (STAC)
  Coverage status : Literature-supported but incomplete
  Note            : The only literature defenses evaluated against STAC (LM-3, LM-4) leave high residual ASR (58.6% / 79.3% at the first attack turn, as reported by the source), so no literature mitigation fully addresses the stateful repeated-C4 sequence; PDM-1 is a project-derived proposal for that residual gap.
  Literature mitigations:
    - LM-3 Harm-Benefit Reasoning [DIRECT] (explicitly evaluated/addressed by the source)
      papers: STAC | timing: Pre-execution (per action)
      effectiveness: Source reports STAC ASR reduced from 87.4% to 58.6% at the first attack execution turn.
      limitations: Residual ASR remains high (58.6%); prompted defenses can be bypassed; effectiveness varies by model.
    - LM-4 Summarization Guardrail [DIRECT] (explicitly evaluated/addressed by the source)
      papers: STAC | timing: Pre-execution (on ingestion)
      effectiveness: Source reports STAC ASR lowered only to 79.3% at turn T (84.7% at T+1, 87.0% at T+2) versus 87.4% / 92.5% / 93.4% with no defense.
      limitations: Largely ineffective against multi-turn stateful sequences; may discard task-relevant detail; injected instructions can survive summarization.
  Project-derived mitigations:
    - PDM-1 Contextual State Transaction Auditing (Stateful Access Control / Sequence Monitoring; Runtime / Pre-execution)
      evidence status: PROJECT-DERIVED — not directly evaluated by the cited literature
      rationale: STAC demonstrates that individually benign state changes can become destructive when combined across turns. SEAgent demonstrates runtime policy enforcement. The project therefore proposes maintaining resource-level state history and requiring confirmation when cumulative operations produce a dangerous state transition.

5. INTERPRETATION LIMITS AND DISCLAIMERS
==========================================================================
- Level-1 static analysis establishes capability presence only. It does not establish tool invocation order, inter-tool data flow, I/O compatibility, or runtime attack execution. This finding is therefore a potential composition and not a confirmed attack path.
- CIA scores are project-derived impact classifications based on consequences demonstrated in the literature. They do not represent attack probability, exploitability, or proof of runtime execution.
- Findings refer to matched literature-backed capability compositions (Level-1 static findings). They are not executed attacks, confirmed attack chains, or observed executions.
- A mitigation applying to a capability does not mean the source paper evaluated it against every attack pattern containing that capability. 'explicitly_addressed' and 'potentially_applicable' are kept distinct; potential applicability is never reported as empirical validation.
- CIA totals are CIA IMPACT TOTALS only; they are not converted into a risk score at this stage.
